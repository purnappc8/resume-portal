How it works

  1. Register your organization with its group email, e.g. hiring@yourcompany.com.
  2. Someone with access to that mailbox confirms it. You become the first recruiter.
  3. Colleagues join with their own username and password. Each request is emailed to the group mailbox to approve or decline.
  4. Each organization has a limited number of recruiter seats.

🔒 Passwords are stored securely (hashed). Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwords. Only signed-in recruiters can contact candidates.
